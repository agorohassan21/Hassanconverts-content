## Evening: Your first cohort doesn't need 1,000 followers. It needs 10 real conversations.

### LinkedIn PDF · 08:00 · Automatic

⬇️ [Download the PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agorohassan21/Hassanconverts-content/main/media/v2/p27b/p27b.pdf)

Document title:
```text
Your first cohort doesn't need 1,000 followers. It needs 10 real conversations.
```

Caption:
```text
“I'll launch when I have more followers.”

How many is enough? 1,000? 10,000? The number keeps moving and the launch never comes.

A first cohort doesn't need a big audience. It needs about ten real conversations: people who asked a real question, whose names, problems and budgets you know.

Where the first ten usually come from:
1. People who commented on your last five posts
2. Old students and old leads in your WhatsApp
3. Friends who know someone with the problem you solve
4. People asking questions under bigger coaches' posts

Followers are an audience. Conversations are a pipeline.
```
