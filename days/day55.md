## Evening: Copy your competitor and you'll always be second.

### LinkedIn PDF · 08:00 · Automatic

⬇️ [Download the PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agorohassan21/Hassanconverts-content/main/media/v2/p55b/p55b.pdf)

Document title:
```text
Copy your competitor and you'll always be second.
```

Caption:
```text
Copying a competitor guarantees second place.

Same fonts, same promises, same “transform your life”. When buyers can't tell two businesses apart, they usually pick the one they saw first.

It's worth studying competitors, then saying what they can't:
1. Your story: why you started
2. Your method: the steps you actually use
3. Your city and your people: the details only you know
4. Your proof: your real results

Be the only one rather than the second best.
```
