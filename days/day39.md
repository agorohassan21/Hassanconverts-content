### LinkedIn PDF · 08:00 · Automatic


Caption:
```text
Nobody reads the second line if the first one is boring.

Some first line patterns that work for small businesses and coaches:
• “(Who you serve), stop scrolling. (Habit) is costing you (loss).”
• “Your (thing they blame) isn't the problem. (Real cause) is.”
• “Tough pill from a (your role): (hard truth).”
• “Steal this: the exact message I send when a customer says (objection).”

Fill the brackets with your customers' own words. Which one would you try first?
```
