## Evening: Lagos traffic teaches patience. Your business needs the same.

### LinkedIn PDF · 08:00 · Automatic

⬇️ [Download the PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agorohassan21/Hassanconverts-content/main/media/v2/p34b/p34b.pdf)

Document title:
```text
Lagos traffic teaches patience. Your business needs the same.
```

Caption:
```text
Nobody turns back at Ojota because the traffic is heavy. You wait, you inch forward, you get there.

With a business, three weeks of posting and no sales is often enough for people to change the offer, the logo, even the niche. And they're back at the start of the traffic.

What works better:
1. Keep the same offer for 90 days
2. Change one thing at a time: the hook, the price, the page
3. Count what happens after each change
4. Talk to 10 people who almost bought

Slow is not the same as stuck.
```
