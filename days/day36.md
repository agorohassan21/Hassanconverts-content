## Evening: Discount or bonus? Do the math before you slash your price.

### LinkedIn PDF · 08:00 · Automatic

⬇️ [Download the PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agorohassan21/Hassanconverts-content/main/media/v2/p36b/p36b.pdf)

Document title:
```text
Discount or bonus? Do the math before you slash your price.
```

Caption:
```text
Black Friday question worth doing the math on: discount or bonus?

Example: a ₦75,000 program at 30% off loses ₦22,500 on every sale. Ten sales and ₦225,000 has been given away. Buyers also learn to wait for the next sale, and the full price starts to look too high.

A bonus that costs time rather than cash (a review call, a template pack) keeps the price intact and still gives a reason to buy before the deadline.

A good bonus: costs little to deliver, is worth a lot to the buyer, speeds up their result, and disappears after the deadline.

Protect the price. Add value instead.
```
