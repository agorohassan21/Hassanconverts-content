## Evening: The business that follows up wins. Not the one that's best.

### LinkedIn PDF · 08:00 · Automatic

Document title:

```text
The business that follows up wins. Not the one that's best.
```

Caption:
```text
In most small businesses, the one that follows up wins, not the one that's best.

After a buyer asks “How much?”, life happens: they get busy, salary is late, they forget. The best provider who never follows up loses to the second best who does.

A simple schedule:
Day 0: answer, then ask one question
Day 1: remove their biggest worry
Day 3: show proof
Day 7: a kind last call

Following up isn't begging. They asked because they wanted help; help them decide.
```
