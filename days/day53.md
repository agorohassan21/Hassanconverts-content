## Evening: The countdown timer that resets is killing your trust.

### LinkedIn PDF · 08:00 · Automatic

⬇️ [Download the PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agorohassan21/Hassanconverts-content/main/media/v2/p53b/p53b.pdf)

Document title:
```text
The countdown timer that resets is killing your trust.
```

Caption:
```text
A countdown timer that resets every time the page loads does more damage than good.

Buyers refresh, watch it start again, screenshot it and share it. After that, every other claim on the page is suspect.

Real urgency is easy to state and costs nothing:
1. A real end date and time
2. The real number of seats
3. The real date the price changes

If none of those are true, don't manufacture one. Sell the result instead.
```
