```text
How I score a first line out of 50
```

Caption:
```text
I score first lines out of 50 before I write the rest of a page.

Five parts, 0 to 10 each. Emotion: does it touch something they feel today? Specificity: numbers, places, prices, names? Curiosity: does it open a question they need answered? Credibility: would a careful buyer believe it? Urgency: why read it now?

Four anonymized scripts I scored before and after a rewrite went from 58 to 91, 61 to 99, 64 to 88 and 57 to 84. Quality scores, not sales numbers.

Which part does your last first line miss?
```
