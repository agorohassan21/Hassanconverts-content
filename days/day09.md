```text
Before you pay any marketer, ask these 7 questions. Including me.

1. What exactly will you deliver, and by when? In writing.
2. How much of my money goes to ads, and how much is your fee?
3. What counts as a lead, a result, a success?
4. Can I pay in stages?
5. Can I see real work you did, live?
6. Who owns the logins, the domain and the ad account?
7. What happens if it doesn't work?

Ask me all 7. I'll answer in writing.
```
