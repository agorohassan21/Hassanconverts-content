# hassanconverts-content

Every approved post for @hassanconverts: slides, LinkedIn PDFs, videos with music, captions and the posting schedule.
Buffer pulls the files from here when a post goes out. Only posts Hassan has approved are added.

## Keep a copy forever

Everything in this repo belongs to your GitHub account (agorohassan21), not to Claude.
It stays here even if a subscription ends.

1. Open github.com/agorohassan21/Hassanconverts-content
2. Tap the green **Code** button
3. Tap **Download ZIP**

On a phone, open the link in Chrome, tap the three dots, then **Desktop site**, and the Code button appears.

## What's inside

**days/** has one page per day (Day 8 to Day 60): tap to download every file, copy every caption. Use it to refill Buffer. Start here.

**plan90.xlsx** (or **plan90.csv**) is the 90 day plan: every post from Day 1 to Day 90 with its hook, what it teaches and its call to action.

**schedule.xlsx** (or **schedule.csv**) is the posting schedule for everything approved so far. One row per post per platform: date, Lagos time, platform, format, which files to upload and which caption file to use.

**media/v2/** holds one folder per carousel (for example d01a). Inside: the slides as JPGs (1080 by 1350, post them in number order) plus the PDF used for LinkedIn.

**media/reels/** holds the swipe videos with music (for example d03a.mp4). Use them for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp status.

**captions/** holds one text file per post: the Instagram caption, the LinkedIn caption, the TikTok and Reel caption, and the post time. Some files have a **NEWS CHECK** line (a quick thing to confirm before a news post goes out) or a **FILM OPTION** line.

**FILM_SCRIPTS.md** has the scripts for the five posts that work best as a video of you (Days 21, 35, 42, 49 and 56). Each one already has a designed version ready, so filming is optional.

## Refill Buffer yourself

GitHub and Buffer don't talk to each other. You are the bridge: download the files from here, then upload them to Buffer. Every day has its own page in the **days** folder with tap to download links and captions you can copy in one tap.

**1. Count the empty spaces.** Open the Buffer app, tap **Publish**, tap Instagram. Count the posts waiting. The free plan holds 10. Ten waiting means full: come back in 2 days. Fewer than 10 means you have space. Check TikTok and LinkedIn the same way.

**2. Find the next post.** Scroll to the last post waiting in Buffer and look at its date. Open the **days** folder here and open the day after it.

**3. Download the files.** Tap the download link on the day page. A video is one link. A carousel is several slides: tap 1, then 2, then 3, in order. LinkedIn is one PDF.
On iPhone, downloads land in the Files app. For a slide, use the "open it here" link, press and hold the picture, then Save to Photos. For a video, open Files, tap the video, tap Share, then Save Video.

**4. Copy the caption.** Each caption sits in a grey box. Tap the copy icon in its corner.

**5. Make the post in Buffer.** Tap **Publish**, then the **+** button. Pick ONE channel. For Instagram, choose **Reel** for a video or **Post** for slides. Paste the caption. Add the video, or the slides in number order.

**6. Choose how it posts.** Instagram and TikTok: tap **Change** and pick **Notify Me**, so you can add a sound. LinkedIn: **Automatic**.

**7. Set the time.** Choose to schedule at a custom time and set the date and Lagos time from the day page. Save.

**8. When Buffer pings you.** Open the notification. Buffer opens Instagram or TikTok with the post ready. Add a trending sound. On Instagram, leave **Share to Facebook** on. Post.

Two rules. TikTok only ever gets the video, never the slides. LinkedIn PDFs are easiest on a computer: go to publish.buffer.com, **Create Post**, pick LinkedIn, paste the caption, **Upload media**, choose the PDF, type the document title, then **Schedule**. No computer? Post the PDF straight from the LinkedIn app at 08:00.

## Schedule it yourself without Buffer (all free)

Open **schedule.xlsx**, pick the next row, then:

**Instagram and Facebook: Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com, or the Meta Business Suite app)
1. Planner, then **Create post** (for carousels) or **Create reel** (for videos)
2. Upload the slides in order, or the .mp4
3. Paste the caption from the captions file
4. Choose **Schedule**, set the date and Lagos time. You can schedule up to 29 days ahead.
To use a trending sound, post the Reel from the Instagram app instead and add the sound there.

**TikTok: TikTok Studio on a computer** (tiktok.com/tiktokstudio)
1. **Upload**, choose the .mp4
2. Paste the TikTok caption
3. Turn on **Schedule** and set the time. You can schedule up to 10 days ahead.
To use a trending TikTok sound, post from the TikTok app on your phone at the time in the schedule.
Never upload the slides as a photo carousel on TikTok. Always use the video.

**LinkedIn** (on a computer or the app)
1. **Start a post**, then **Add a document** and upload the PDF from the post's folder
2. Give the document a title, paste the LinkedIn caption
3. Tap the **clock** icon to schedule. You can schedule up to 3 months ahead.

**Buffer free plan** also works: 3 channels, up to 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time. Upload the same files and paste the same captions.
