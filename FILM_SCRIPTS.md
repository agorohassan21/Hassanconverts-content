# Film scripts

Five posts in Days 16 to 60 work best as a real video of you. Each one already has a designed version ready in media/v2 and media/reels, so nothing is left blank. If you film, post your video instead of the designed one, at the same time, with the same caption.

## Day 21 evening, Sun 18 Oct: Lagos traffic taught me copywriting

Length: 35 seconds. Designed backup: media/v2/p21b/ and media/reels/p21b.mp4. Caption: captions/p21b.txt

**How to film:** Film on your phone, vertical, in traffic or at a bus stop. Face to camera for the first and last line; the middle can be clips of the road with your voice over. Morning or evening light, no need for a mic if the road isn't too loud.

**What to say:**

> Lagos traffic taught me more about copywriting than any course.
>
> Watch the conductor. He doesn't shout “nice bus”. He shouts the destination: “CMS! CMS!” That's your headline. Tell people where they're going.
>
> The hawker has three seconds between cars. He shows the product, says the price, collects the money. No “DM for price”.
>
> And if the bus door is jammed, it doesn't matter how loud the conductor shouts. That's your checkout.
>
> Say where they're going. Show the price. Open the door.
>
> Follow for more Lagos Logic.

**Shots:**

1. You, face to camera, by the road: the first line.
2. A danfo or conductor calling passengers (from a distance, no faces needed).
3. A hawker between cars, or goods on a tray.
4. A bus door opening, or people boarding.
5. You again, face to camera: the last two lines.

**Text on screen:** Lagos traffic taught me copywriting

## Day 35 evening, Sun 1 Nov: I lost a $500 job because of ONE word

Length: 40 seconds. Designed backup: media/v2/p35b/ and media/reels/p35b.mp4. Caption: captions/p35b.txt

**How to film:** Face to camera, vertical, somewhere quiet. Say the first line like a confession, then slow down for the lesson. Optional: show a phone screen with a proposal on it while you talk.

**What to say:**

> I lost a five hundred dollar job because of one word.
>
> A big copywriter I respect sent me a job link. My portfolio was good. I knew I could do the work.
>
> The job post said: start your proposal with the word FUNNEL, in capital letters. It was their test, to see who actually read everything.
>
> I read it. I understood it. And I forgot it. I opened with my pitch.
>
> That was it. Job gone.
>
> The small instruction is the test. Your buyers do this too. They ask one simple question in your DM. Answer that question first. Then pitch.

**Shots:**

1. You to camera: the first line, no smile.
2. Phone screen: a job post (blur any names).
3. You to camera: 'I read it. I understood it. I forgot it.'
4. You writing a list: read twice, highlight every instruction.
5. You to camera: the lesson.

**Text on screen:** I lost a $500 job because of ONE word

## Day 42 evening, Sun 8 Nov: Things I will NEVER do for a client

Length: 30 seconds. Designed backup: media/v2/p42b/ and media/reels/p42b.mp4. Caption: captions/p42b.txt

**How to film:** Face to camera, calm and firm. Count the three on your fingers.

**What to say:**

> Things I will never do for a client, even for money.
>
> One: write fake testimonials. If you don't have reviews yet, we show the work and the guarantee.
>
> Two: fake scarcity. No timer that resets. No 'two slots left' when there are twenty.
>
> Three: promise a cure.
>
> Trust is the product. A lie can win one sale. It loses every sale after it.

**Shots:**

1. You to camera, counting 1, 2, 3.
2. Close up for the last line.

**Text on screen:** Things I will NEVER do for a client

## Day 49 evening, Sun 15 Nov: How a sales page gets made in Lagos

Length: 40 seconds. Designed backup: media/v2/p49b/ and media/reels/p49b.mp4. Caption: captions/p49b.txt

**How to film:** Five short clips across one real working day, vertical. Voice over or text on screen.

**What to say:**

> How a sales page gets made in Lagos.
>
> Morning: research. Every DM, every comment, every review. The exact words buyers use.
>
> Afternoon: write, then cut. Ten headlines. Keep one.
>
> Evening: the light goes. Power bank, hotspot, keep going.
>
> Before it goes live: I open it on a cheap phone with weak network, and I try to pay.

**Shots:**

1. Laptop with notes and screenshots of comments (blur names).
2. Notebook with ten headlines, nine crossed out.
3. Room goes dark, power bank and hotspot on.
4. A cheap phone loading the page on mobile data.
5. You to camera: 'Follow for behind the work.'

**Text on screen:** How a sales page gets made in Lagos

## Day 56 evening, Sun 22 Nov: What I'd tell myself on day 1

Length: 40 seconds. Designed backup: media/v2/p56b/ and media/reels/p56b.mp4. Caption: captions/p56b.txt

**How to film:** Face to camera, reflective. One tip per cut.

**What to say:**

> What I'd tell myself on day one of copywriting.
>
> One: read the brief twice. I once lost a five hundred dollar job over one word.
>
> Two: your buyer's words beat your clever words. The best lines are already in their DMs.
>
> Three: show your thinking, not your numbers.
>
> Four: learn to put the page live yourself.
>
> What would you tell your day one self?

**Shots:**

1. You to camera for each of the four points.
2. Optional: a quick shot of a live page on your phone for point four.

**Text on screen:** What I'd tell myself on day 1
