INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

Things I will never do for a client, even for money:

1. Write fake testimonials.
2. Fake scarcity: no resetting timers, no “2 slots left” when there are 20.
3. Promise a cure.

Trust is the product. Everything else is packaging. A lie can win one sale. It loses every sale after it.

Follow if you build like this.
