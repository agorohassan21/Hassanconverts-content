LINKEDIN CAPTION

A Black Friday tip that works better than a surprise sale: open a waitlist three weeks before.

A surprise sale sells to whoever happens to be awake. A waitlist sells to people who already raised their hand.

The message: “I'm opening a Black Friday offer for [program] on 27 November, only for people on the list. It'll be the best price I do this year, plus a bonus I won't offer again. Want me to add you? Reply YES.”

Then: thank them with the exact opening time, remind them two days before, send the link to the list first, and one kind reminder on the last day.

The list is the launch.
