LINKEDIN CAPTION

A free class WhatsApp group is a classroom. Most of them look abandoned.

No welcome message. No rules. The last message in the group: “Is the class still holding?” So people mute it, and then they leave.

A simple setup fixes most of it: a pinned welcome, three rules, the date and time repeated, a reminder schedule, and a planned close after the class, with the offer and a deadline.
