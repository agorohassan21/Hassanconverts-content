INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

I lost a $500 job because of one word.

A big copywriter I respect sent me a job link. My portfolio was good. The job post said: start your proposal with the word FUNNEL, in capital letters. It was their test to see who read everything.

I read it. I understood it. I forgot it. I opened with my pitch instead.

The small instruction is the test. Your buyers do it too: one simple question in the DM. Answer that question first, then pitch.

What's the most expensive small mistake you've made?
