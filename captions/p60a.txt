Say thank you to the last 5 people who bought from you. Watch what happens.

Most businesses chase new buyers all year and forget the old ones. The people who already paid you trust you the most.

Send: “Just wanted to say thank you for trusting me with [offer]. How is it going for you now?”

Often what comes back: a testimonial, a referral, a second purchase.

Comment SENT when you've sent one.
