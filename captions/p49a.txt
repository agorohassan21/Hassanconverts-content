LINKEDIN CAPTION

Three signals from September about where selling online is heading:

1. Meta launched Muse, an AI agent for small businesses, in the US and Canada (Meta newsroom, Axios). Not in Nigeria yet, but tidy ad accounts and clear offers will matter more as these tools arrive.
2. Paystack launched onsite card checkout for Shopify stores in South Africa. One Shopify agency said some clients saw “an increase in conversion of up to 15%” from that feature alone (IT Online). Fewer redirects, more buyers.
3. Nigeria's economy grew 4.43% in Q2 2026, with services up 4.60% (NBS, via Tribune and P.M. News). Useful context, not proof every customer has more to spend.
