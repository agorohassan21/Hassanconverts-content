INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

Friends not supporting your business? Stop doing these 3 things.

1. Begging for likes in the group chat.
2. Selling to them first. Friends are rarely your customers.
3. Taking their silence personally. Most are just busy.

Build for strangers who need what you sell. Your customers will come from people you haven't met yet.

Tag the friend who always shows up.
