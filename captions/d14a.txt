LINKEDIN CAPTION

Before you run an early bird discount, do this 30 second math.

Example: a ₦100,000 program. Each student costs ₦50,000 to get, counting ads, tools and support time. Profit: ₦50,000.

Take 25 percent off. Price: ₦75,000. Cost stays ₦50,000. Profit: ₦25,000. You now need 2 students to make what 1 made.

A bonus call, a pay in 2 option or early access can do the job without cutting your margin. Value first. Discount last.
