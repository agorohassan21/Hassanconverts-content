INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

Your partner is your first clarity test.

Ask them tonight: “So what exactly do I sell?” If the person who loves you can't explain your offer in one breath, a stranger scrolling at 11pm won't buy it.

The one breath test: who it's for, what they walk away with, how long it takes, what it costs.

Try it, then tell me what they said. Be honest.
