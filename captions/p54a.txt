TIKTOK / REEL CAPTION

9 things to check before you post your Black Friday offer:

1. The price is visible.
2. The deadline has a date and time.
3. It says who it's for.
4. The bonus or saving is real.
5. One button, same words everywhere.
6. Payment works on a phone with weak network.
7. Proof near the top.
8. Refund promise in one line.
9. Someone replies to DMs within the hour.

LINKEDIN CAPTION

A nine point check before posting a Black Friday offer:

1. The price is visible, not hidden behind a DM
2. The deadline has a date and a time
3. It says who it's for, in one line
4. The bonus or saving is real
5. One button, with the same words everywhere
6. Payment works on a phone with a weak network
7. There's proof near the top
8. The refund promise fits in one line
9. Someone will reply to messages within the hour

Every “no” is a buyer who leaves on the busiest day of the year.
