Three things from recent news worth checking before Black Friday:

1. Google announced a report for AI Max search campaigns that shows the customer's search, the ad they saw and the landing page in one view, rolling out later in 2026 (BusinessDay, BrandSpur).
2. Google's AI Brief lets advertisers steer campaigns with plain language instructions such as “never mention prices”. Olumide Balogun, Google's Director for East and West Africa: “Every naira matters, especially in the weeks before Black Friday.” (BusinessDay, Pulse Nigeria, BrandSpur)
3. Adobe expects traffic from AI tools to US retail sites to rise 130% this holiday season (Adobe, Forbes). US data, but a signal worth noting: more buyers are asking AI what to buy, so offer pages that state who, what, price and date plainly are easier to find and quote.
