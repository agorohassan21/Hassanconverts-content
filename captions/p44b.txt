INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

For some Facebook Pages, link posts are becoming a paid feature.

Meta launched Meta One on 15 September: paid business plans from $14.99 a month (Meta newsroom, Forbes). The Advanced plan includes “links in organic posts and reels”, and some Pages are in a test that limits link posts to two a month (Social Media Today).

Renting your audience is getting more expensive. Build roads you own: DMs, WhatsApp numbers, emails.
