LINKEDIN CAPTION

Three numbers from September worth thinking about before setting Black Friday prices in Nigeria:

1. Headline inflation eased to 15.39% in August, from 23.14% a year earlier (NBS, via Nairametrics and Premium Times). Prices are still rising, just more slowly.
2. The CBN cut the monetary policy rate from 26.5% to 23% on 22 September (Nairametrics, Tribune). Nearly a week later, banks had not passed the cut on to lending rates.
3. The naira held in a narrow band of about ₦1,327 to ₦1,330 per dollar through September (Nairametrics).

Buyers remain price conscious. Leading with results and payment plans will likely serve better than deep discounts. Worth checking the newest figures before you post prices.
