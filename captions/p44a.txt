LINKEDIN CAPTION

A Black Friday post I see some version of every November:

“BLACK FRIDAY MEGA SALE!!! 50% OFF EVERYTHING!!! Limited slots!!! DM for details!!!”

Four leaks:
1. No offer: off what, exactly?
2. No trust: how many slots, until when?
3. Friction: the price and link are hidden behind a DM
4. No urgency: no end date or time

The rewrite: “Black Friday: my 6 week program, ₦75,000, plus a free 1:1 review call and my template pack. 20 seats. Ends Monday 30 November, 11:59pm. Link in bio.”

Same offer, four fixes, and the price stays whole. (Composite example, not a real business.)
