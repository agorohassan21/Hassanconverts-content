TIKTOK / REEL CAPTION

How to answer “Can you reduce the price?” without cutting it.

A vague price invites bargaining. Make it feel final: write the rule once, show the bigger option first, give 3 choices. Make it feel fair: say what's included, trade instead of cutting, walk away kindly.

Trade. Never cut. Every cut teaches your buyers to ask again.

LINKEDIN CAPTION

“Can you reduce the price?” lands in every coach's inbox.

Cutting the price once teaches buyers to ask every time. Trading works better: keep the price, add something that costs you little and means a lot to them, like a bonus call, early access or a template pack.

Example: “The price is fixed at ₦75,000. But if you join by Friday, I'll add a 1:1 call in week 1.” (price is an example)
