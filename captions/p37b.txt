NEWS CHECK: confirm Meta hasn't changed WhatsApp service message pricing again since October.

INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

Since 1 October, WhatsApp charges businesses for replies on the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Meta now charges for service messages, which had been free since November 2024 (Meta for Developers). Each number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, then replies are charged at country rates (Mobile Ecosystem Forum, respond.io).

This hits API tools and chatbots, not the free WhatsApp Business app on your phone.

If you use a tool: check your volume and put your price and FAQs on one page instead of 20 messages.
