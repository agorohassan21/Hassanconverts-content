INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

I write sales pages for herbal teas. There's one word I will never write: cure.

It breaks NAFDAC's advertising rules for herbal products, and buyers have heard it too many times. Overclaiming can win one sale. It loses the second.

Coaches, “guaranteed results” is your “cure”. Promise what you control, and let your students' own words carry the results.
