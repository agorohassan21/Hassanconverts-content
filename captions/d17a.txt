LINKEDIN CAPTION

Six coaching phrases that promise everything and prove nothing:

“Unlock your full potential.” “Take your business to the next level.” “Are you tired of being tired?” “Link in bio for more info.” “Guaranteed results.” “Join thousands of successful students.”

Replace each with something a buyer can picture or check: a result, a time frame, a price, a start date, a refund they understand, students in their own words.

Which one would you retire first?
