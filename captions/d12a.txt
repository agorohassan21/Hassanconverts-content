Day 12 A: Stop writing “DM for details”
Fri 9 Oct 2026, Instagram 07:30

INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Stop writing “DM for details”.

Write who it's for, what they get, the price, one proof and a keyword to DM. Serious buyers get their answer, and the DM becomes about their start date, not your price.

Swipe for 5 examples: a free masterclass, 1:1 coaching, a group program, fitness coaching and consulting. Prices are examples.
