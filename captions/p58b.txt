INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

Course creators: the Lagos tax agency is testing a 5% withholding tax on creator payouts.

It started with a dispute with Selar over a backdated 5% charge (TechCabal, BusinessDay). LIRS said: “Where royalty is paid to an individual… the applicable withholding tax rate under the WHT Regulations is 5%,” and called it an open verification exercise. It isn't settled.

Don't panic. Prepare: keep records of every sale and payout, get your tax ID, price with a small buffer.
