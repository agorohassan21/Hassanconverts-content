INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

The tools I use to go from research to a live page:

Research: AI research assistants to gather and sort what buyers say, and the buyers' own DMs, comments and reviews.
Writing: a plain document first. No design yet.
Building: a page builder so I put it live myself.
Testing: a cheap phone, the way buyers see it.

Tools don't write. They save time. The thinking is still yours.
