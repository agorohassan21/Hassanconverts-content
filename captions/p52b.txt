INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

Stop making your partner your unpaid social media manager.

“Babe, like my post.” Every day. Their likes don't pay rent.

Ask for real support instead: honest feedback (“Would you understand this if you didn't know me?”), one introduction to someone who needs what you sell, and quiet when you're working.

Tag the partner who has liked every post.
