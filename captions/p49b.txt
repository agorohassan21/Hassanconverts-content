How a sales page gets made in Lagos, light and data and all:

Morning: research. Every DM, comment and review, and the exact words buyers use.
Afternoon: write, then cut. Ten headlines, keep one.
Evening: the light goes. Power bank, hotspot, keep going.
Before it goes live: open it on a cheap phone with weak network and try to pay.
