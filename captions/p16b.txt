INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

Last month, the CBN asked a question about “pay small small”.

At Nigeria Fintech Week, CBN official Abiodun Okunola said instalment plans from non banks are in effect credit: “They are offering credit… they are not licensed to offer credit. How do we regulate?” (Nairametrics, Technext)

It's a question, not a ban. No new rule or deadline. But if you sell in instalments: write your terms plainly, keep records, and watch for official guidance.
