How to say sorry when work made you miss something important.

Not “You know how busy I am.” That's a defence, not an apology.

Own it: “I missed it. That's on me.” Name it: “I know it mattered to you.” Fix the next one: “It's in my calendar now.”

Then keep the promise. An apology without a change is just a speech.
