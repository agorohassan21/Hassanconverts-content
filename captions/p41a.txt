Working with your spouse? 4 rules before you destroy your marriage.

1. Separate roles. One person owns each decision.
2. A work day end time. After it, no business talk.
3. Disagree in private, never in front of staff or clients.
4. Celebrate every win out loud.

The marriage came first. Protect it like your best client.
