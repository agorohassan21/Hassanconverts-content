LINKEDIN CAPTION

“Sales are slow this month.”

Most of the time that sentence really means: I don't know where last month's sales came from.

When nothing is counted, a good month feels like luck and a bad month feels like a curse. Both are just numbers nobody wrote down.

Three things worth counting, starting this week:
1. One question on every signup form: where did you hear about us?
2. One keyword per post or ad, so you know which one brought each buyer.
3. One sheet you fill every Sunday: who asked, who paid, where they came from.

After four Sundays you know exactly what to do more of. That's not luck. That's a business.
