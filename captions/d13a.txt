“How much is the program?” is the most important message in a coach's inbox, and a bare price often ends the conversation.

A better first reply gives the price, the value and one question: “₦75,000 for 6 weeks: 12 live classes, all recorded. What made you ask about it today?” (price is an example)

The rule for every reply after that: end with a question or a next step. Never leave the ball in their court with nothing to hit.
