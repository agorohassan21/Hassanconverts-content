INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

Lagos traffic taught me more about copywriting than any course.

The conductor shouts the destination: that's your headline. The hawker shows the product and price in 3 seconds: no “DM for price”. And a jammed door beats the loudest conductor: that's your checkout.

Say where they're going. Show the price. Open the door.
