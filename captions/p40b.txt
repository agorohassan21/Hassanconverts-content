INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL CAPTION

I used to brag about how many scripts I'd written. Here's why I stopped.

“I've written a lot of scripts” sounded impressive to me. Then everyone online had a bigger number. Numbers stopped meaning anything.

Buyers don't want to know how much you've done. They want to see how you think: one page, one decision on it, and the buyer words it came from.

What do you lead with: numbers or thinking?
